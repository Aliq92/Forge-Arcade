Choose Cable and drag from a generator or router's round port to another port. Power follows the arrows. Tap a source then a destination, or use the notebook's keyboard connection controls.

Each building needs its full demand. Cables and substations have limits: a striped cable or warning means demand cannot fit. Open switches interrupt the route. Damaged cables need a new route around them.

Batteries have three modes: Off, Charge from a generator, or Support the city. Energy changes only during an explicit stability check or sandbox simulation. Finish the required hold to wake a district.

Earn a star for completion, another for no overload warnings during the attempt, and a third for a clean attempt within the cable budget. Reset clears warning history. Planning is untimed. Undo keeps energy already used; Reset restores the puzzle.

Drag empty ground to pan. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Arrow keys pan, +/− zoom, F fits, U undoes, Escape pauses. Use C/S/B/I to choose a tool. Press Hint (H) for an exact source → destination connection; follow the gold FROM and TO markers. Hints never cost stars.
