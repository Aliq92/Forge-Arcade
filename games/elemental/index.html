Facts, models and limits

Reference data: sourced numerical records and experimental geometries. Predictions are labelled separately. “Not established” means unavailable in this dataset snapshot, not necessarily unknown to all science.

Simplified models: shell counts, orbital boxes, Lewis electron accounting and ball-and-stick depictions. These do not simulate quantum motion, energies, reaction feasibility or synthesis.

Validation domain: only the nine verified reference connectivities are SUPPORTED. MODEL WARNING means additional chemistry is needed. INVALID WITHIN MODEL flags a defined bookkeeping or domain violation, not a universal impossibility claim. Radicals, resonance, hypervalence and coordination need more nuanced models.

Oxygen’s usual O=O Lewis drawing omits its triplet ground state and paramagnetism. Ammonia’s conventional molecular formula is NH₃; its Hill-ordered formula is H₃N. Element colours and ball sizes are display conventions.

Primary references

  • PubChem periodic table — configurations, classifications and optional properties.
  • CIAAW standard atomic weights 2024 and radioactive isotope mass numbers. A bracketed interval is a standard-weight range; a single bracketed integer is a representative isotope mass number.
  • IAEA LiveChart — isotope stability and half-lives. Natural abundances are omitted rather than assumed constant.
  • NIST CCCBDB, SRD 101 — experimental molecular Cartesian coordinates, in Å.
