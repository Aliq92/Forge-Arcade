How To Play

Tap empty space to place a gravity well.
Tap a well to flip it between attract (gold) and repel (coral).
Drag a well to reshape the garden around it.
Hold a well to remove it.

Gold wells pull particles in. Coral wells push them away. Particles reveal the invisible field between them — mix attract and repel to grow petals, streams and rings.

Use the Particles tool to seed, burst, stream, ring or cloud new particles. Use More for speed, trails, color, and field visualization.
