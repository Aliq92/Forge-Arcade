Welcome, Operator

You're piloting a tiny tracked excavator. Use A/D or the on-screen arrows to move left and right through cleared tunnels.

If terrain blocks your path, moving into it starts digging automatically.

Down (S) digs the tile beneath you. Up (W) digs upward if blocked, or activates your winch to climb a shaft that's already clear — your only way back to the surface.

Holding a dig direction digs continuously. Different materials take longer.

Gravel and stone can become unstable when undermined. Watch for cracks and falling dust — you'll have time to move away or press F to drop a support that stabilizes the area.

Cave-ins are always telegraphed. You'll never be caught with zero warning.

Watch your Fuel and Hull — they only drop while you move, dig, or take damage. Return to the Depot on the surface to sell ore, refuel, repair, and upgrade.

Goal: recover 3 ancient fragments, then bring the assembled discovery back to the depot.
