CALIBRATE YOUR INSTINCT

Aim. Strike. Repeat.

Your robot stays at the center. Move the mouse, drag the arena, or use A/D and ←/→ to aim. Click the arena, press Space, or tap STRIKE for one energy pulse. Holding does not repeat attacks.

Targets must be inside your directional wedge and within reach. The thin inner timing band marks a Perfect Hit: more points, more energy and a musical accent. A normal hit is still useful.

△ SPARK Straight approach. One hit.

◇ DRIFTER Gentle sideways drift. Track it.

⬡ BULWARK Two hits. Watch the shell crack.

□ SPLINTER Breaks into two smaller sparks farther out.

A whiff or escaped target breaks your combo, unless Combo Keeper protects it. An escaped target costs a shield; brief invulnerability prevents a pile-up of damage. Three shields to start. No automatic restart.

Levels advance automatically after a short breather. Choose one upgrade every three levels. The Forge Heart returns every ten levels, telegraphing its opening before moving into strike range. There is no final level. Press Escape to pause; hiding the tab pauses too.
