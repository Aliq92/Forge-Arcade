Build. Push. Tinker. Repeat.

Fill the gaps using your gadget box. Fixed toys have a lock. Drag a toy to move it, or enter its coordinates in the inspector. Set up the entire machine before pressing PUSH.

Click a tool then the tabletop to place it. On touch screens, choose a tool and drag or tap to place. Select and rotate with the arrow buttons. Drag empty space to pan, pinch or scroll to zoom.

R / E rotates clockwise; Q rotates back. Delete removes a toy. Ctrl / ⌘ Z undoes, Shift Z redoes. Space pushes. P pauses. Use Tab and the inspector for keyboard editing.

Gears and switches connect to gears or springs through visible links. Gears use a simplified coupling rule, not simulated teeth. Springs are single use and launch only a nearby toy. Seesaws respond to real weight. A moving toy must touch the finish.

Fewer placed toys earn more stars. Slow motion never changes the physics. Replay shows recorded snapshots at 15 Hz; it cannot resume a simulation from a scrubbed frame.
