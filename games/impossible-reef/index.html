    The living ocean

    Garden: Tap the water to plant. Tap a coral to select, then nurture or remove it. Two different mature species near each other may crossbreed. Your original six species and garden save remain available.

    Survey: Pilot the golden research probe with WASD, arrow keys, or tap a destination. Reach glowing treasures and the four hidden habitats. A ring marks a rare pearl. All regions are open from the start.

    Drifting chevrons show the current direction. You can always swim against it. Bubble vents lift for two seconds in every six. The harmless coral curtain opens four seconds in every eight; wait for OPEN or swim around it. Brushing it only affects the optional clean-discovery badge.
