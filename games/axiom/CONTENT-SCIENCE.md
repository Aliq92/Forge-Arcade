# AXIOM curriculum and equation review

The content pack contains 50 grouped lessons, five in each of ten disciplines. Each lesson has named-topic definitions, an intuitive explanation, a worked mathematical relation and a distinct advanced discussion of derivation or scope. This is an introductory structured survey with selected deeper cases, not a replacement for a full graduate course. Laboratory links distinguish what is implemented from guided reading: the oscillator, Higgs potential and holographic entropy discussions do not masquerade as general numerical solvers.

The public data exports match `API.md`: `SUBJECTS`, `LESSONS`, `CONNECTIONS`, `THEORIES`, `PARTICLES`, `DISCOVERIES`, `EQUATIONS` and `REFERENCES`. At completion there are 186 defined topics, 78 labelled graph edges, 14 theory comparisons, 17 measured Standard Model particle entries, 10 historical milestones, 44 equation entries and 27 references. The 29 scalar calculators return finite values with SI units and use the shared strict parameter normalizer and physical constants. They reject unknown keys, nonfinite inputs and invalid joint domains. Missing input keys use displayed defaults. Math units and notation are Unicode text suitable for accessible native rendering.

## Mathematical scope

All four Maxwell laws have differential and integral forms, source/field units and orientation assumptions. Fixed-loop Faraday induction is separated from motional EMF. The Ampère–Maxwell displacement-current term is retained. Gauss laws constrain flux rather than supplying a universal field solver.

Einstein's tensor equations and the Schrödinger operator equation have guided solvable examples, definitions and limits; neither has a fictitious scalar calculator. The weak-field/vacuum Schwarzschild example is distinct from an arbitrary-matter metric solution. The infinite box is nonrelativistic, normalized and idealized; the oscillator discussion explicitly states its different spectrum. Tunnelling describes probability current without energy borrowing. Neutrino oscillations state the two-flavour vacuum approximation and both the SI phase and conventional-unit phase factor.

The scalar examples include force, gravity, displacement, momentum, kinetic energy, angular momentum, torque, spring period, gas pressure, energy transfer, entropy, Carnot efficiency, Coulomb force, resistance, perpendicular magnetic force, RC charging, wave speed, Snell refraction, thin lenses, acoustic Doppler, time dilation, rest energy, Schwarzschild radius, box energy, spin probability, uncertainty bound, decay expectation, cosmological wavelength and circular speed. The lens focus returns an explained invalid finite result; it does not replace infinity by a large made-up number. Total internal reflection similarly has no real transmitted angle. Carnot requires a hotter source than sink, and acoustic motion remains subsonic. For the scalar calculator's declared conservative domains, Newtonian gravity requires compactness 2G(m₁+m₂)/(rc²) < 0.01; circular speed requires v < 0.1c; box energy requires En < 0.01mc². Constant-acceleration displacement also requires both endpoint speeds below 0.1c; because velocity is linear in time, that bounds speed throughout the interval. These are explicit pedagogical validity cutoffs, not new boundaries of the physical theories.

## Evidence and particle properties

Classical mechanics, SR, GR, quantum mechanics and the Standard Model are established within their stated domains. QFT's evidence is the success of specific theories rather than a claim that every possible field theory is physically true. String theory and M-theory prominently say **UNCONFIRMED PHYSICAL FRAMEWORK**. Supersymmetry, loop quantum gravity, specific inflation mechanisms and proposed holographic descriptions are not presented as confirmed descriptions of our universe. The holography discussion distinguishes internal evidence for specific theoretical dualities from empirical evidence about cosmology. Mathematical consistency is not experimental confirmation.

Dark-matter gravitational evidence is separated from its unknown identity. Cosmic acceleration and the useful cosmological-constant parametrization are separated from dark energy's unknown physical origin. The inflation lesson contains established horizon mathematics but marks the proposed early-universe mechanism as unconfirmed. Quantum interpretations are separated from tested predictions, and measurement never requires consciousness.

Particle charges are in units of elementary charge, spin in units of reduced Planck constant, and listed teaching masses in MeV/c². Quark notes qualify confinement, renormalization scheme and scale; top mass uses a qualified collider scale. The W± entry explains both charges rather than assigning both species +1. Photon/gluon zeros describe the Standard Model mass parameter with experimental/confinement qualifications. The three flavour-neutrino masses are `null`: flavour is not a mass eigenstate and oscillation splittings do not specify three individual absolute masses. Hypothetical superpartners do not appear in the measured-particle list.

The timeline records scientific history, never user achievements. GW150914 distinguishes its September 2015 observation from its February 2016 publication. The Higgs milestone distinguishes the cautious July 2012 announcement from later property measurements.
