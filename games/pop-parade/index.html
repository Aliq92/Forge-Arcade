A little strategy. A lot of confetti.

Tap a balloon. Its shockwave pops nearby balloons of the same color, and each one sends out another wave. Bigger chains earn more per pop. Lightning reaches farther through the same color. Freeze holds nearby balloons still for 2.8 seconds. Rainbow receives and sends shockwaves of any color.

Classic: three taps per round; missed taps don’t count. A full clear earns 2,000 bonus points. Zen: unlimited taps and fresh balloons, for as long as you like.

Keyboard: arrow keys select a balloon, Enter or Space pops it. P pauses, M mutes, R restarts.
