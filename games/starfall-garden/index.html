HOW TO PLAY

Walk near falling fragments to collect starlight.

01 · Simple controls

MoveWASD / arrowsDrag the stick on mobile.
DashSpaceTap DASH to dodge a warning ring.
RestoreE on dead groundOr tap RESTORE. Costs 2✦ in Gentle.
Plant1–4, then ETap a species, then PLANT on restored soil.

02 · Give every plant a purpose Base costs

Benefits begin at maturity. In Gentle, every species spreads restoration; Glowgrass spreads twice as fast.

03 · Bring the sanctuary to life

  1. Restore 25% of the planet.
  2. Grow all four species to maturity.
  3. Survive one full starfall night.
  4. Finish at 100% with six mature living plants, including all four species.

The first three milestones stay completed and each reward 4✦ + 8 health. Open Sanctuary Journey to see your next step.

No rush in Gentle. Fragments keep falling. Scorched ground regrows, unlocked regions stay open, and your health recovers in calm and recovery.
