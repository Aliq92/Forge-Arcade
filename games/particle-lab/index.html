Field guide & keyboard controls

Materials occupy one cell each. Warm ice melts at 0°C; water boils at 100°C; steam condenses below 75°C. Lava sets into stone below 650°C. Wood and oil burn. Water can extinguish fire. Metal spreads heat quickly.

Brush / line / filled rectangle add to empty spaces; erase removes editable matter. Heat and cool change temperatures without adding material. Fill replaces a connected empty region. Tools work while paused. Challenge fixtures are outlined and cannot be edited; some experiments restrict tools, places and budgets.

Space pause · . step · B L R F E H C I tools · Ctrl Z undo in sandbox · Esc cancel gesture / close panels. On the canvas, arrow keys move a cursor and Enter applies a tool. Simulation is simplified, deterministic and resolution independent.
