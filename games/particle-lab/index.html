Controls & field notes

Draw: drag a material or the Particles tool.
Place: choose a field and tap the canvas.
Move: select any field and drag its centre.
S: select · P: particles · A: attractor · R: repulsor · V: vortex · B: rebirth well
Space: pause · Delete: selected field
Esc: close controls

Rebirth wells hold up to 64 particles each. They release one particle for each absorbed, after a short delay, with 0.8 seconds of protection from reabsorption. Up to eight fields and 1,500 light particles. Queued particles count toward the total.
