Field guide & keyboard controls

Warm ice melts at 0°C. Water boils at 100°C and steam condenses at 75°C. Lava sets into stone at 650°C. Wood and oil burn. Water extinguishes fire, and metal spreads heat quickly.

Brush, line and rectangle add to empty spaces. Fill replaces connected air. Heat and cool change temperature. Challenge fixtures are marked at the edges and cannot be edited.

Space pause · . step · B L R F E H C I tools · Ctrl Z undo · Esc cancel. On the canvas, arrow keys move the cursor and Enter applies the tool.
