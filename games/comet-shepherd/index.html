HOW TO PLAY

Momentum

Your comet drifts forever on its current heading unless gravity or a correction changes it. You never steer directly — you nudge.

Course Correction

Drag anywhere in the sky, then release to thrust in the drag direction. Arrows / WAD give precise nudges; S or B gently brakes. On touch screens, hold the directional buttons. Corrections spend energy and scale down when energy is low.

Trajectory Preview

Press E to toggle the projected path. It follows moving planets and your currently held controls; dragging previews the affordable impulse. Red marks warn of a collision. Future input changes the forecast.

Gravity Assists

Skim close to a planet without colliding and its gravity will whip you onto a faster new heading. Watch for the SLINGSHOT feedback.

Ice & Heat

Sunlight melts you. Ice Integrity is your health — it drops from heat, collisions and hazards. Stay cold, collect ice fragments, and mind the CRITICAL warning.

Gems & the Gate

Collect eight gems to finish a sector: cyan diamonds +10, amber hexagons +30, violet triangles +50. Choose an upgrade and continue, or explore and use the gate. The helmeted chicken is an optional +150 bonus. Retry a sector from Pause without losing earlier sectors.

CHALLENGE

A seed always generates the same system chain. Share it, or replay it to sharpen a route.
